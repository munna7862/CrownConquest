# Crown & Conquest — Game Manual & Player Guide

## 1. Overview
Crown & Conquest is a deterministic, local-first hybrid RTS/RPG set in a dynamic medieval realm. Command vast armies across five distinct civilization eras, harvest resources, construct fortified citadels, recruit and level up heroic commanders, and lead individual soldiers who gain persistent combat veterancy on the battlefield.

---

## 2. Core Game Controls & Keybindings

| Input | Action |
|:---|:---|
| `Left Click` | Select unit, hero, building, or resource node |
| `Left Click + Drag` | Box selection of multiple military units |
| `Right Click (Ground)` | Move selected units to location |
| `Right Click (Enemy)` | Attack target unit or building |
| `Right Click (Resource)` | Assign workers to gather resource |
| `Shift + Right Click` | Queue movement or action waypoint |
| `1` – `9` | Select assigned control group |
| `Ctrl + 1` – `9` | Assign selected units to control group |
| `F1` – `F4` | Trigger active hero abilities |
| `Space` | Jump camera to recent battlefield alert |
| `Tab` | Cycle through active unit types in selection |
| `Esc` | Cancel selection / Open pause menu |

## 3. Economy & The 5-Resource Model

Economy drives military recruitment, castle fortification, and technological advancement:
1. **Food:** Harvested from farms, berry bushes, and wildlife. Essential for recruiting workers, infantry, and cavalry.
2. **Wood:** Chopped from forests. Required for archers, siege engines, palisades, and economic buildings.
3. **Gold:** Mined from gold veins. Powers mercenary recruitment, hero abilities, and advanced technologies.
4. **Stone:** Quarried from stone outcrops. Used for stone walls, gatehouses, defensive towers, and castles.
5. **Iron:** Extracted from iron deposits. Fuels heavy armor upgrades, swordsmen, knights, and siege rams.

## 4. Signature Mechanic: Individual Unit Progression & Veterancy

Unlike traditional RTS games where units are expendable identical clones, every combat unit in Crown & Conquest earns battlefield experience (Kill XP) for striking down enemy combatants:

$$\text{Enemy Defeated} \longrightarrow \text{Kill XP Awarded} \longrightarrow \text{Auto Level-Up} \longrightarrow \text{Veterancy Rank} \longrightarrow \text{Stat & Visual Polish}$$

### Veterancy Ranks:
- **Level 1–2 (Recruit):** Base unit stats.
- **Level 3–4 (Experienced):** $+10\%$ Health, $+5\%$ Attack Damage, $+1$ Armor. Bronze rank badge.
- **Level 5–6 (Veteran):** $+25\%$ Health, $+15\%$ Attack Damage, $+2$ Armor, $+10\%$ Movement Speed. Silver rank badge.
- **Level 7–8 (Elite):** $+45\%$ Health, $+25\%$ Attack Damage, $+3$ Armor, $+15\%$ Morale aura. Gold rank badge.
- **Level 9+ (Legendary):** $+70\%$ Health, $+40\%$ Attack Damage, $+5$ Armor, Fear resistance, Crown heraldry.

## 5. Factions & Playstyles

1. **Kingdom of Valoria (Feudal Chivalry):**
   - Signature Units: Royal Knights, Longbowmen, Paladins.
   - Strengths: Unstoppable cavalry charges, heavy stone castles, chivalric morale bonuses.
2. **Ironclad Dominion (Imperial Engineering):**
   - Signature Units: Armored Crossbowmen, Siege Bombards, Iron Vanguard.
   - Strengths: Rapid iron gathering, reinforced fortification armor, devastating siege weapons.
3. **Sylvan Enclave (Guerrilla Woodland Guardians):**
   - Signature Units: Woodland Rangers, Druidic Wardens, Swift Huntresses.
   - Strengths: Forest camouflage, unmatched movement speed, rapid ambush strikes.

## 6. Civilization Eras & Technological Progression

Advance your civilization through five historical eras:
1. **Dark Age:** Basic foraging, wooden clubs, and primitive watchtowers.
2. **Feudal Age:** Town centers, blacksmithing, leather armor, and archer ranges.
3. **Castle Age:** Heavy stone keeps, knight stables, siege workshops, and universities.
4. **Imperial Age:** Plate armor, ballistas, trebuchets, and faction mastery techs.
5. **Renaissance / Industrial Dawn:** Gunpowder bombards, elite royal guards, and grand cathedrals.
