# Phase 04 — Civilization

## Objective
Expand the settlement into a civilization progression system inspired by classic RTS games.

## Era Progression
Initial placeholder structure:
- Tribal Age
- Kingdom Age
- Imperial Age
- Dominion Age

Final names belong to the original game lore.

## Technologies
Examples:
- Iron Forging
- Reinforced Shields
- Horse Breeding
- Siege Engineering

Technologies modify data-driven stats and capabilities.

## Buildings
Expand:
- Archery Range
- Stable
- Blacksmith
- Academy
- Market
- Fortress
- Tower
- Walls
- Gate

## Units
Add:
- Archer
- Spearman
- Cavalry

## Faction Modifiers
Introduce:
- Economy bonuses.
- Military bonuses.
- Technology bonuses.
- Building bonuses.

## Deliverables
The player can progress through eras, research technologies, unlock new buildings and produce multiple unit classes.

## Tests
- Technology prerequisites.
- Research cost.
- Research completion.
- Technology modifiers.
- Era prerequisites.
- Unlock conditions.
- Faction modifiers.
- Production availability.

## Definition of Done
Civilization progression creates meaningful strategic choices rather than being only a visual upgrade.

## Exit Criteria
The player has a functioning economic, technological and military progression path.
