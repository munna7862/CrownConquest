# Phase 05 — Heroes

## Objective
Introduce the RPG layer and make heroes central battlefield commanders.

## Hero State
Each hero has:
- Unique ID
- Name
- Level
- XP
- Attributes
- Abilities
- Equipment
- Reputation
- Wounds
- Battle history

## Hero Progression
- XP
- Level-up
- Skill points
- Ability unlocks
- Equipment progression

## Abilities
Initial categories:
- Leadership
- Combat
- Strategy
- Defensive
- Offensive
- Utility

## Auras
Heroes can affect nearby units.

Examples:
- Morale bonus.
- Attack bonus.
- Defense bonus.
- Movement bonus.
- Faction-specific effects.

## Hero/Unit Relationship
Heroes should:
- Command groups.
- Improve nearby troops.
- Influence morale.
- Participate directly in combat.

## Wounds
Future-ready status model:
- Healthy
- Injured
- Severely Wounded
- Recovering

## Death
Campaign mode may support permanent hero death depending on game rules.

## Tests
- Hero XP.
- Ability activation.
- Cooldowns.
- Aura radius.
- Aura stacking.
- Hero death.
- Wound state.
- Save/load progression.

## Deliverables
At least one fully functional hero integrated with units and combat.

## Definition of Done
The hero changes how a battle is fought rather than acting as a normal unit with higher statistics.
