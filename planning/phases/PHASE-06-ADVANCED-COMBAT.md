# Phase 06 — Advanced Combat

## Objective
Turn the basic combat system into a tactical battlefield.

## Formations
Initial:
- Line
- Column
- Defensive
- Wedge
- Shield Wall

Formations modify:
- Unit positioning.
- Movement.
- Defense.
- Attack.
- Vulnerability.

## Unit Classes
Establish counter relationships without relying exclusively on rock-paper-scissors.

Factors:
- Unit type.
- Armor.
- Weapon.
- Terrain.
- Formation.
- Morale.
- Experience.
- Hero effects.

## Morale
States:
- Inspired
- Confident
- Stable
- Shaken
- Panicked
- Routing

Morale inputs:
- Nearby deaths.
- Hero presence.
- Enemy strength.
- Victory streak.
- Defeat.
- Being surrounded.
- Terrain.

## Terrain
Initial:
- Grassland
- Forest
- Hill
- Mountain
- River
- Road
- Swamp

Terrain affects movement, visibility and combat.

## Cavalry
Implement:
- Charge.
- Speed.
- Anti-ranged role.
- Anti-cavalry counters.
- Formation interaction.

## Ranged Combat
Implement:
- Range.
- Accuracy.
- Projectile travel.
- Line of sight.
- Target priority.

## Siege
Initial:
- Ram
- Catapult
- Ballista
- Walls
- Gates
- Towers

## Tests
- Formation modifiers.
- Morale transitions.
- Terrain modifiers.
- Cavalry charge.
- Ranged line of sight.
- Siege damage.
- Wall destruction.
- Routing behavior.

## Definition of Done
Battle outcomes depend on tactical decisions, not only raw unit statistics.
