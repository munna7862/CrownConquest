# Phase 08 — Campaign

## Objective
Connect individual RTS battles into a strategic world and campaign.

## World Map
Regions contain:
- Settlements.
- Villages.
- Mines.
- Forests.
- Forts.
- Ruins.
- Roads.
- Rivers.
- Mountain passes.

## Strategic Armies
An army contains:
- Units.
- Heroes.
- Supplies.
- Experience history.
- Position.
- Orders.

## Battle Transition
When opposing armies meet:
1. Determine battlefield.
2. Generate tactical context.
3. Load RTS battle.
4. Resolve combat.
5. Return survivors and progression to world state.

## Territory
Territory can affect:
- Resources.
- Recruitment.
- Movement.
- Visibility.
- Economy.
- Strategic routes.

## Missions
Initial mission types:
- Destroy enemy base.
- Defend settlement.
- Escort army.
- Capture location.
- Survive.
- Eliminate commander.
- Control resources.

## Story
The original world should have:
- Factions.
- Leaders.
- Heroes.
- Conflicts.
- Historical timeline.
- Regional lore.

## Save/Load
Campaign state must preserve:
- Territory.
- Units.
- Unit XP.
- Unit kills.
- Heroes.
- Technologies.
- Resources.
- Relationships.
- Mission progress.

## Definition of Done
The player can progress through multiple connected missions while retaining meaningful army and hero progression.
